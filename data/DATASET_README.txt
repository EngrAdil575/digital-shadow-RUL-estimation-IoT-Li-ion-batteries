Datasets of the manuscript
"AI-Driven Digital Shadow for Health Monitoring & Remaining Useful Life
 Estimation of IoT-Connected Li-Ion Batteries"

Both datasets are public. They are NOT redistributed in this repository; obtain
them from their original sources below.

1. NASA Battery Data Set (cells B0005, B0006, B0007, B0018)
   B. Saha and K. Goebel, "Battery Data Set," NASA Prognostics Data Repository,
   NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA, 2007.
   NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE) Data Set Repository:
   https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/
   U.S. Government work, public domain.
   Usage: download the four .mat files and place them in your Google Drive
   (Colab) or in the data folder (DATA_ROOT, default ./data) on your own machine.

2. MIT-Stanford-Toyota LFP dataset (124 lithium iron phosphate cells)
   K. A. Severson, P. M. Attia, et al., "Data-driven prediction of battery cycle
   life before capacity degradation," Nature Energy 4, 383-391 (2019).
   doi:10.1038/s41560-019-0356-8
   Available at: https://data.matr.io/1/
   Usage: Part C of the notebook downloads the three batch files automatically
   (several GB). No manual step is required.

Please cite the original sources above if you use either dataset.
