Monitoring dashboard of the digital twin
A Charging-Data Digital Twin for IoT-Connected Lithium-Ion Batteries: Health, Charge and Remaining-Life Estimation with Calibrated Uncertainty and Tolerance to Sensor Faults
Packed on 2026-11-01.

twin_dashboard.html
  Open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). It needs no internet connection and no Python.
  It replays the recorded tests cycle by cycle and shows, for each cell, the estimated state of health,
  the remaining useful life with its calibrated 90% band, the state of charge, alerts and the latest readings.
  Two views: the four NASA cells, and a selection of LFP cells.
  Use the play button or the slider to move through the cycles, and click a cell to see its charts.
